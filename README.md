# MINTED

MINTED is an interactive storyboard game designed to teach financial literacy in a fun and engaging way. Players navigate through multiple finance-themed stories, answer questions, and compete with friends or online users. The game tracks performance and provides a detailed summary to help players learn from their mistakes.

## Overview

MINTED is designed to make financial education interactive and social. By combining storytelling, quizzes, and competitive elements, players not only learn key financial concepts but also engage with peers in a friendly learning environment.

The game emphasizes multiple storylines that explore different financial topics, such as:

- Budgeting and Saving

- Investing and Stock Market Basics

- Cryptocurrency and Digital Finance

## Features
1. Voice Recognition for Finance Terms

- Users can speak financial terms or concepts into the app.

- The app detects these terms and generates relevant stories or mini-games.

- Example: Saying “stocks” could generate a scenario-based story about investing.

2. Interactive Story Gameplay

- Each detected term leads to a finance-related story.

- Players navigate through decisions and challenges in a “choose your path” style.

- Correct choices earn points, while incorrect choices provide explanations to help learning.

3. Quizzes and Knowledge Checks

- Stories are followed by short quizzes related to the topic.

- Immediate feedback shows where players went wrong and reinforces correct concepts.

4. Multiplayer and Online Play

- Play solo or with friends.

- Compete online to see who earns the most points and ranks higher on the leaderboard.

5. Progress Tracking

- Points earned are tracked across sessions.

- Summaries at the end of each game highlight strengths and areas for improvement.

## How to Play

1. Open the app and allow microphone access.

2. Speak a finance-related word (e.g., “budget,” “cryptocurrency”).

3. Explore the story generated based on your term.

4. Make decisions and answer questions within the story.

5. Earn points and check your progress on the leaderboard.

6. Review the summary to understand mistakes and correct concepts.
